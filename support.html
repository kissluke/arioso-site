Arioso Support

A practice mirror for the piano

Arioso listens while you play an acoustic or digital piano and shows you what you actually played — not what you meant to play. It is deliberately not a game: there are no streaks, no scores to chase, and nothing to beat.

Common questions

What do I need to use it?

A piano — acoustic, digital, or hybrid — and your iPhone or iPad nearby with microphone access allowed. Headphones on a digital piano will prevent Arioso from hearing you, so use its speakers.

It isn't hearing me, or it hears notes I didn't play

Put the device closer to the instrument and away from the floor. Quiet the room if you can — fans, traffic, and other music all reach the microphone. Check that microphone access is allowed in Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone.

I bought a book and it still looks locked

Open the book's cover and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account you bought it on. Purchases belong to that account, so they follow you to a new device.

My progress isn't on my other device

Progress syncs through your own private iCloud. Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud Drive on, and iCloud enabled for Arioso in Settings › [your name] › iCloud. Syncing can take a minute or two.

Can I print the music?

Yes. Pieces in the purchasable books include engraved sheet music you can open and print from the piece screen.

How do I delete my data?

There is a reset option in the app's Settings that erases everything Arioso has stored.
